# What the creators advise
*Counts are the number of videos (out of 55) that gave each piece of advice.*

## Hooks (17 videos)
- **Use a hook structure (7).** The versions given were:
  - pose a question
  - call-out + open loop + pain or benefit
  - specific viewer + dream result + solution
  - wide appeal, emotion, curiosity gap and authority
  - Drop in → Solve it → Give them somewhere to go
  - stack a proof or dream hook right after the main hook (2)
  - Best lines: “The first thing out of your mouth should never be an intro.” “Always ask a question at the end of your first sentence.”
- **Borrow what works (8).** Three videos gave fill-in templates (“You are doing this wrong”, “[X] won't fix your [Y]”, “No (thing) needed”). Five said to take hooks and formats from videos with 100K+ views or from outliers.
  - One video disagreed: “If you are copying hooks in 2026, you're probably already starting off behind the ball… You want principles.”
- **Visual hooks (4).** Lie down, slam a book shut at the lens, walk into an empty frame, or toss an object. Also: “Double the number of b-roll clips in the first two seconds.”
- **Speed (2).** “You have 1 to 3 seconds to get people to watch your video and 7 seconds to keep them there.” “Cut that first half second out.”

## Scripting and story (16)
- **Story and emotion (8).** Methods given:
  - The storytelling wheel (Love, Identity, Fitness, Earnings): “ask yourself why three times” to move a story from safe to real to raw.
  - Stakes → big question → head fake → re-hook.
  - Rotate educational, emotional and entertaining videos.
  - “Provide value or entertainment every video.”
  - Best line: “That last level is what makes viewers actually feel something, connect to you and trust you.”
- **Talk to one specific viewer (5).** “The one thing you actually need to know is simply who your viewer is.” “It's okay to reach less people.” Make content for yourself two to three years younger.
- **Be controversial (3).** “If you have a hot take, then get it out there.” Debates in the comments grow the video.
- **Relatable beats expert (3).** “Being an expert… actually gets you less views.” “Post the thing no one can take from you.”
- **One video each:** use simple words; find share-worthy news with Google Alerts.

## Filming (8)
- Put the light in front of you and use a mic (2).
- Film a new clip per sentence. Switch hands each sentence, or “film a new clip for every new sentence” (2).
- Film anywhere on just your phone, handheld; “leave the bloopers in” (1).
- Framing: eyes high, captions lower-middle, keep clear of the bottom and right edges, keep the eyeline level on punch-ins, add a stroke to text on busy backgrounds (1).
- Batch filming into one day, use a shot list, bank b-roll, show your face (2).

## Editing (21)
- **Cut dead space and get to the point (7).** “People's attention spans are shot, so keep the pauses out and keep quick transitions between thoughts.”
- **Captions (7).** Use auto captions, 1–2 lines, inside the viewable area. The “SLC” rule: short, large, clear and high-contrast. Fast-changing captions keep the screen moving.
- **Sound effects (4).** Click or pop for pop-ups, whoosh for transitions, flash for quick cuts, glitch for text, riser for tension, record scratch for hooks, bell ding for CTAs.
- **Music by mood (4).** Pick tracks for cinematic, funny, motivational or emotional videos. Keep TikTok's suggested trending music.
- **Edit with restraint (2).** “Not too much editing, just enough.” “Stop over editing.”
- **One video each:**
  - Add text before anything else, and keep fonts consistent.
  - Use keyframes for smooth zooms and text that follows you.
  - Read the retention graph: an early drop means the hook failed, a steady decline means the value failed, a late drop means attention failed.

## Posting and growth (12)
- **Post more, consistently (8).** “Post again, post again, and post again.” “Just post, shut up, and forget.” “Do more, then better, and then new.” Ignore hashtags, posting time and SEO, and stop checking analytics.
  - One video disagreed: “You don't have a posting frequency problem, you have a posting strategy problem.”
- Repost flops with changes, and create series instead of vlogs (1).
- Study viral videos and “steal from a world you got no business in” (2).
- “Improve ONE thing with each video” (1).

## Business (5)
- Have an anchor offer, mention it a few times a week, and aim content at the viewer who buys it (2).
- “People buy into people.” “Selling to those people isn't being salesy.”
- Trust is “earned with honesty”, not a staged set (2).
- Guru content is built to make you feel deprived; focus on your own goal (1).

## How an automatic editor can apply this
- **Hook check before render.** Score the first sentence for a number, a call-out, a result, an open question and a pain or benefit. If any are missing, offer 3 rewrites in the user's own words plus the proven templates. Flag greetings and auto-trim the dead first half-second.
- **Proof prompts.** Detect spoken numbers and results, ask the user to upload the matching screenshot, and drop it in on the spoken word.
- **Saved viewer profile.** Ask once for the ideal viewer and the offer. Reuse them to write call-outs, titles and comment-keyword CTAs, and to remind users to mention the offer weekly.
- **Story helper.** When a script reads flat, run the “why × 3” prompt and suggest stakes and head-fake beats.
- **Default captions.** Use SLC style, 1–2 lines, placed automatically off the overlays, with a stroke on busy backgrounds. Generate the text hook first.
- **Sound and music presets.** Map effects to events: pop on text, whoosh on overlays, riser into reveals, ding on the CTA. Pick music by detected mood and keep it ducked under the voice.
- **Filming coach on upload.** Warn about backlighting, low voice level and a static single take. Suggest switching hands or locations per sentence, filming on the go, and banking b-roll.
- **Visual-hook suggestions** for the first 2s: walk-in, toss, b-roll burst.
- **Retention feedback.** Read connected analytics, map early, mid and late drop-offs to hook, value and attention fixes, and auto-generate a re-edit with a new hook for reposting.
- **Cadence tools.** Batch-edit a day of clips into a week of posts, auto-label series (“Part 2”), and queue reposts of flops with fresh hooks.
